## Assembly Guide

### 1. Pan Servo & Neck Assembly

Insert the bottom pan servo into the circular aperture located on the upper part of the base. Fasten it securely using screws through the two designated mounting holes.

Press a standard servo horn onto the pan servo shaft. Screw the two-axis neck structure directly onto the servo horn so that it rotates along with the motor shaft.

### 2. Tilt Servo & Head Mount Assembly

Place the tilt servo into the mounting slot on the neck structure and secure it with screws through the corresponding mounting holes (or with adhesive, if necessary).

Screw or glue the upper head plate directly onto the tilt servo.

### 3. Sensors & Electronics Mounting

**LED:** Press-fit the 5 mm LED into the front circular aperture until firmly secured.

**Capacitive Touch Sensor:** Attach the sensor to the front interior face using screws through the four pre-modeled mounting holes.

**Buzzer:** Mount the buzzer to the side wall of the base using screws through the two mounting holes.

**Ultrasonic Sensor:** Insert the sensor into the cutouts designated for the "eyes" on the corresponding mount, and glue the mount to the top plate of the head support.

### 4. Wiring & Enclosure Integration

Place the Arduino board and internal wiring inside the main cavity of the base, aligning the USB port with the side opening provided for access.

Align the lower base plate with the main body and join them using the press-fit joint designed for assembly.
